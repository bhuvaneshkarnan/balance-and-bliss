Liver Qi Stagnation and Foot Tendon Tightness: Harmonizing the Free Flow of Vital Energy

In classical medicine, 'The Liver governs the tendons and stores the blood.' When chronic frustration, anger, or stress stagnates Liver Qi, your physical tendons become brittle, tight, and prone to chronic spasms and heel stiffness.

Liver 3 (Taichong) is the premier acupressure point for harmonizing Liver Qi and relaxing tight tendons.

The Great Surge: Liver 3 (Taichong) on the Dorsum of the Foot

Located on the top of the foot in the hollow between the first and second metatarsal bones, Liver 3 (Taichong - 'Great Surge') is arguably the most powerful anti-stress point in the entire meridian system. It is the Yuan-Source point of the Liver channel.

Releasing Emotional Frustration and Physical Rigidity

Applying sustained pressure to Liver 3 instantly smooths stagnant Qi throughout the body. Patients experience a sudden deep breath, their shoulders drop away from their ears, and rigid plantar fascia and Achilles tendons immediately soften.

Clinical Recommendation: Press Taichong When Stressed

Whenever you feel overwhelmed or your muscles feel like coiled springs, spend 2 minutes pressing between your big toe and second toe. It releases the body's master stress valve.

Resolving Headaches, Eye Strain, and PMS

Because the Liver meridian ascends all the way to the vertex of the head and encircles the eyes, balancing this foot point also relieves temple headaches, red strained eyes, and menstrual irritability.

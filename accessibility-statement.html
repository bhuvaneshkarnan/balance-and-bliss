At Balance and Bliss Healing Therapies and Academy, we are committed to providing accessible digital and physical healthcare environments for all individuals, including people with disabilities, chronic mobility impairments, and special accessibility requirements.

Digital Web Accessibility

Our website has been designed adhering to modern accessibility guidelines (WCAG 2.1 level AA). Key accessibility features implemented include:

  • High-contrast warm typography ensuring readability across all devices.
  • Descriptive alternative text tags on clinical and informational imagery.
  • Full keyboard navigability for interactive buttons, links, and forms.
  • Clear semantic heading hierarchies and screen-reader accessible elements.
  • Responsive mobile layouts with finger-friendly touch targets.

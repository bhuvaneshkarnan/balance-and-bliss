The 90-Degree Vascular Kink: Trapping Blood at the Inguinal Crease

When you sit in an office chair, your hips and knees are bent at 90-degree angles. This posture kinks the femoral artery and vein at the inguinal crease like a bent garden hose, reducing venous return by over 70% and causing calf swelling and spinal disc compression.

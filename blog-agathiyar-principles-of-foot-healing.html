Sage Agathiyar's Gunavagadam: Ancient Tamil Verses on Plantar Health and Longevity

Sage Agathiyar, the father of Tamil literature and Siddha Medicine, dedicated numerous verses in treatises like 'Agathiyar Gunavagadam' and 'Agathiyar Nayana Vidhi' to the paramount importance of the feet in determining human lifespan.

Dr. M. Mehraj Abdullah, M.D (Acu) ATAMA Reg. No: 20261714 / TN • May 14, 2027 • 6 min read
Sage Agathiyar's ancient verses laid the foundational principles for drugless foot therapy.

'Feet Kept Warm, Head Kept Cool': The Golden Verse

Agathiyar famously proclaimed: 'கால்களை வெதுவெதுப்பாகவும், தலையைக் குளிர்ச்சியாகவும் வைத்திருப்பவனே நோயற்ற வாழ்வைப் பெறுவான்' (He who keeps his feet warm and his head cool enjoys a life free of disease). In modern physiology, this describes ideal autonomic vascular regulation.

The Foot as the Mirror of the Three Humors (Mukkutram)

Siddha medicine evaluates the Three Humors: Vatham (air/ether), Pitham (fire), and Kabam (water/earth). Agathiyar taught that Vatham lodges in the bones and feet. Daily foot oiling (Padha Abhyanga) subdues erratic Vatham, preventing 80 types of nervous disorders.

Clinical Wisdom: Oil Your Feet Every Night

Agathiyar taught that massaging the soles before sleep preserves eyesight, calms the mind, and ensures long life. Make foot care your foundational daily health ritual.

Evidence-Based Non-Invasive Orthopedics: Medical Studies Supporting Clinical Reflexology

Far from being an unverified folklore practice, clinical reflexology is supported by a growing body of rigorous medical research, functional MRI brain scans, and randomized controlled clinical trials worldwide.

Dr. M. Mehraj Abdullah, M.D (Acu) ATAMA Reg. No: 20261714 / TN • June 09, 2027 • 6 min read
Peer-reviewed medical trials increasingly confirm the measurable neurological benefits of reflexology.

Functional MRI Evidence: Proof of Somatotopic Mapping

Groundbreaking fMRI neuro-imaging studies (such as those published by Nakamaru et al.) demonstrate that stimulating the foot reflex zone corresponding to the eye or small intestine activates the exact corresponding somatosensory and visual cortical areas in the brain in real time.

Randomized Controlled Trials for Pain and Neuropathy

Clinical trials published in prestigious international journals confirm that standardized reflexology protocols produce statistically significant reductions in chronic low back pain, diabetic peripheral neuropathic burning, and post-operative pain scores compared to control groups.

Scientific Fact: The Brain Confirms Reflexology

Modern brain-scanning technology proves what practitioners have known for centuries: foot reflex stimulation produces real, measurable changes in cerebral and organ function.

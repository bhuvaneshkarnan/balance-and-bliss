Zero Ulcer Protocol: Preventing Diabetic Foot Breakdown Through Early Neurovascular Warning Signs

The Fatal Triad: Neuropathy, Ischemia, and High Pressure

Diabetic ulcers do not occur by accident. They develop from a lethal combination: sensory neuropathy (patient feels no pain), microvascular ischemia (tissues heal slowly), and mechanical pressure peaks beneath prominent metatarsal heads or calluses.

Identifying Pre-Ulcerative Hotspots Early

During each clinical session at Balance & Bliss, Dr. Mehraj inspects the feet for localized erythema, temperature spikes, or deep tissue induration. By applying gentle reflexology around these zones, pressure concentrations are redistributed across the entire sole.

Doctor's Golden Rule: Prevention Is Everything

Never wait for an open wound to appear. Regular clinical foot care and microcirculation maintenance stop ulcers before they ever break the skin.

Zero Amputations: A Commitment to Madurai's Diabetic Community

By combining regular clinical reflexology sessions with patient education on daily self-inspection and seamless footwear, our patients maintain intact, healthy, ulcer-free feet year after year.

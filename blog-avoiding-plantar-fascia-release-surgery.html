What Happens When You Cut the Plantar Fascia?

The plantar fascia is the primary tie-rod of your foot's windlass mechanism. When a surgeon cuts it, tension on the heel bone drops, but the entire medial arch collapses. This destabilizes the midtarsal joints, creating severe lateral foot pain, cuboid syndrome, and accelerated knee arthritis.

Why 92% of Cases Resolve Conservatively

Medical consensus confirms that over 90% of chronic plantar fasciitis cases resolve without surgery when treated with proper biomechanical decompression. At Balance & Bliss, our integrated hydrotherapy and reflexology protocol restores elasticity without cutting tissue.

Clinical Rule: Surgery Is Irreversible

Once a ligament is cut, it can never be un-cut. Exhaust genuine, certified clinical drugless reflexology before considering invasive surgical procedures.

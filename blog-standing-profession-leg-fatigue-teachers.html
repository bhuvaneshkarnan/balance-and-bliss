The Cumulative Trauma of Prolonged Static Standing

Static standing is actually more harmful to lower extremity circulation than walking. Without the dynamic contraction of the calf muscles, venous blood pools in the feet under continuous gravitational pressure, while the plantar fat pad is flattened and compressed.

The Restorative Three-Step Clinical Protocol

At Balance & Bliss in K.K Nagar, we treat standing professionals with warm herbal hydrotherapy to restore tissue warmth, followed by deep cross-fiber stripping of the compressed plantar fascia and upward venous drainage strokes to clear toxic metabolic fatigue.

Doctor's Advice: Cushion Your Feet and Change Footwear

Invest in supportive, cushioned shoes with wide toe boxes and rotate between two different pairs on alternate days. Clinical reflexology will do the rest to keep your legs pain-free.

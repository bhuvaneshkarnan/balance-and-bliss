Biomedical Rationale

Why Non-Drug Medicine Works

Pharmaceutical analgesics and muscle relaxants often act as chemical blunts, masking pain receptors in the brain without resolving underlying mechanical nerve impingement or visceral inflammation. Over time, this can lead to tolerance, gastric irritation, and organ fatigue.

01

Neurological Reflex Arcs

The human foot contains over 7,000 sensitive nerve endings embedded in myofascial tissue. In Foot Reflexology, precise somatotopic compression sends afferent signals through the spinal cord, modulating efferent nerve signals that regulate internal organs, vascular tone, and muscle relaxation.

02

Endorphin & Gate Control

Classical Acupuncture stimulates specific low-electrical-resistance meridian points. This sends impulses along large myelinated nerve fibers that close the "pain gate" in the dorsal horn of the spinal cord, while triggering the pituitary-hypothalamus complex to release natural beta-endorphins and serotonin.

03

Spinal Decompression & Heat

Intervertebral discs require movement and negative hydrostatic pressure to draw in nutrients and hydration. Our automated thermal jade bed provides gentle motorized axial traction coupled with far-infrared wavelengths, decompressing spinal nerves and restoring natural vertebral spacing.

Patient Guidelines

Preparing for Your First Consultation

To ensure the most comfortable and effective treatment experience at Balance and Bliss, please review these simple preparation guidelines:

1

Wear Comfortable Clothing

Wear loose pants or clothes that can easily roll up above your knees and elbows to allow unrestricted access to foot and arm reflex acupoints.

2

Light Meal Ahead of Time

Have a light meal or snack 1 to 2 hours before your visit. Avoid arriving completely fasted or immediately after a very heavy, greasy meal.

3

Bring Existing Reports

If you have recent X-rays, MRI scans, or blood work related to your condition (sciatica, disc bulge, thyroid, ultrasounds), bring them along for Dr. Mehraj to review.

4

Allow 60–75 Minutes

Your initial diagnostic visit includes thorough pulse analysis, foot reflex assessment, and initial therapy. Plan for an unhurried, peaceful session.

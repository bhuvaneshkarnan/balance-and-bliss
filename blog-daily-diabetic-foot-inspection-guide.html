Diabetic Foot Care & Neuropathy • Clinical Guide

The Complete Daily Diabetic Foot Self-Care Routine Recommended by Dr. Mehraj Abdullah

Taking care of your feet when you have diabetes is not complicated, but it must be consistent. Here is the doctor-prescribed 5-minute daily routine that protects your feet from ulcers, infections, and complications for a lifetime.

Dr. M. Mehraj Abdullah, M.D (Acu) ATAMA Reg. No: 20261714 / TN • December 23, 2026 • 6 min read
A 5-minute daily inspection mirror routine prevents 90% of diabetic foot complications.

Step 1 & 2: The Mirror Inspection and Lukewarm Wash

Every evening, sit in good light and use a small hand mirror to inspect the underside of your heels, arches, and between each toe for redness, cuts, blisters, or calluses. Wash feet daily with mild soap in lukewarm water (always test water temperature with your elbow, never your foot).

Step 3 & 4: Thorough Drying and Smart Moisturizing

Gently pat dry with a soft cotton towel—never rub aggressively. Pay special attention to drying completely between the toes to prevent fungal tinea pedis. Apply a good moisturizer to the tops and soles of your feet, but NEVER apply cream between the toes where trapped moisture causes skin maceration.

Doctor's Daily Checklist: 5 Minutes That Save Your Limbs

Inspect daily, wash gently, moisturize the soles, and never walk barefoot. Simple daily vigilance is your most powerful safeguard against diabetic foot complications.

Step 5: Inspecting Footwear and Never Walking Barefoot

Always shake out your shoes before slipping your feet in to ensure no stones or pins are inside. Never walk barefoot—not even inside your home on marble or tiled floors—as unseen splinters or burns from hot floors can lead to painless ulcers.

The plantar arch holds direct somatotopic reflex maps for the stomach, liver, and intestines.

The Enteric Nervous System: Your Second Brain

The gut contains over 500 million neurons—more than the spinal cord—operating as the enteric nervous system (ENS). It constantly communicates with the central nervous system via the vagus nerve and thoracolumbar splanchnic pathways.

Mapping the Digestive Tract on the Plantar Arch

The instep and central arch of both feet mirror the anatomy of your digestive tract: the stomach and duodenum on the upper arch, the small intestine in the central hollow, and the ascending, transverse, and descending colon tracing a continuous horseshoe across both soles.

Clinical Key: Treat the Root of Digestion

Digestion is governed by your autonomic nervous system. Calming intestinal reflex zones on your feet switches your gut into restful assimilation mode.

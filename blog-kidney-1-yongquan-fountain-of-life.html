Kidney 1 (Yongquan - ஊற்றுக்கண்): The Most Grounding Acupressure Point on the Human Sole

Located in the depression formed when the foot is flexed—at the junction of the anterior and middle thirds of the sole—lies Kidney 1 (Yongquan, meaning 'Gushing Spring' or 'ஊற்றுக்கண்'). It is the lowest, most grounding acupuncture point on the human body.

Dr. M. Mehraj Abdullah, M.D (Acu) ATAMA Reg. No: 20261714 / TN • January 20, 2027 • 6 min read
Kidney 1 (Yongquan) serves as the primary grounding gateway connecting the body to earth energy.

The Root of the Water Element and Ancestral Jing

In Classical Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine, the Kidney meridian stores 'Jing' (vital ancestral essence) and governs the Water element. Kidney 1 is the Wood point on a Water channel, acting as a dynamic portal that draws excess pathological heat and mental agitation downward out of the head.

Clinical Applications: From Hypertension to Severe Panic

Dr. M. Mehraj Abdullah utilizes sustained, deep thumb pressure on Yongquan to treat chronic insomnia, severe anxiety, high blood pressure spikes, and tinnitus. Activating this point stimulates parasympathetic nerve tone, grounding racing thoughts within seconds.

Clinical Key: Ground Your Energy Downward

When modern stress sends heat, anger, and hypertension rushing to your head, stimulate Kidney 1 on your soles. Pulling energy down to the earth restores instant calm.

Daily Self-Care Routine for Madurai Residents

Massaging Kidney 1 with warm gingelly (sesame) oil for 3 minutes before bedtime is an ancient, time-tested Tamil ritual that promotes profound, dreamless sleep and restores morning vitality.

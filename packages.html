Common Questions

Questions About Care Plans & Sessions

The initial consultation covers an in-depth 45-minute clinical evaluation directly with Dr. M. Mehraj Abdullah, M.D (Acu). This includes pulse examination, tender reflex point palpation on the feet, spinal alignment analysis, a review of your previous diagnostic scans or medical reports, and an honest clinical discussion of your treatment roadmap.

For acute pain (such as a recent back spasm or neck stiffness), significant relief is frequently noticed within 2 to 4 sessions. For long-standing chronic conditions (such as sciatica nerve pain, severe disc bulges, chronic gastritis, or varicose veins), a staged series of 8 to 12 sessions under our Chronic Care Protocol is typically recommended to stabilize the healing reflex.

Acupuncture needles are hair-thin, flexible, and completely solid (unlike hollow injection needles). Most patients experience only a mild pinch or an energetic sensation resembling a gentle tingle or warmth (called "De Qi"). We use exclusively 100% single-use sterile disposable needles in sanitized treatment cubicles.

We accept all standard digital UPI payment methods (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) as well as cash. Receipts are provided for all consultation and treatment packages.

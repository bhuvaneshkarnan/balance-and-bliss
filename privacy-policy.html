The information and content provided on this website are for educational and informational purposes only. While our therapies are aimed at supporting health, activating the body's natural restorative abilities, and providing relief for functional and chronic ailments, individual response and physiological recovery times vary according to age, lifestyle, condition severity, and constitutional vitality.

Our treatments do not replace emergency medical treatment, major surgery, or emergency care. Patients under medical treatment or taking prescribed medications should never discontinue their medications without the guidance of their physician.

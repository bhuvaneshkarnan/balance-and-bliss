The Mechanical Trap of the Hiatal Diaphragm

The esophagus passes through an opening in the diaphragm (the esophageal hiatus) before entering the stomach. When chronic emotional stress or shallow breathing causes the diaphragm muscle to cramp, it spasms against the lower esophageal sphincter, forcing acid upward into the throat.

The Plantar Diaphragm Line Reflexology Technique

A prominent horizontal line runs directly across the sole beneath the ball of the foot, representing the respiratory diaphragm. Dr. Mehraj uses deep, stretching thumb strokes along this line to release chronic diaphragm contractures, allowing the stomach to settle back down into its natural anatomical position.

The Vital Role of the Liver in Hormone Metabolism

The liver is responsible for metabolizing active estradiol into weaker metabolites (estrone and estriol) and conjugating them for excretion via bile. If the liver is sluggish, estrogen is reabsorbed into the bloodstream, creating relative progesterone deficiency.

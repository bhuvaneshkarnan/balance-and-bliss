Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS): Recharging Cellular ATP Energy Without Stimulants

For individuals suffering from Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (ME/CFS), standard exercise programs cause devastating 'post-exertional malaise' (PEM). Discover how passive, gentle reflexology recharges cellular energy without depleting fragile reserves.

The Mitochondrial Energy Deficit in CFS

CFS is characterized by mitochondrial dysfunction: cells fail to efficiently produce adenosine triphosphate (ATP) through aerobic cellular respiration, forcing tissues into inefficient anaerobic glycolysis that produces overwhelming cellular fatigue and lactic acidosis.

Passive Healing: Zero Energy Expenditure Required

Unlike active exercise, clinical reflexology is completely passive. While the patient rests comfortably, Dr. Mehraj stimulates the spleen, liver, and adrenal reflex zones, enhancing microvascular oxygen delivery to tissues without triggering post-exertional crashes.

Clinical Rule: Never Push Past Your Energy Envelope

In chronic fatigue, pushing through exhaustion damages cellular repair. Gentle, consistent reflexology supports your mitochondria safely without causing crashes.

Rebuilding Stamina Step by Step

Over a structured 8-week protocol, patients slowly expand their energy envelope, transitioning from being bed-bound to enjoying daily walks and social activities.
